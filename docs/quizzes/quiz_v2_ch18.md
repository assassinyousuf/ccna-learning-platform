- D. Scattering

### Question 2: Which term refers to the erratic spreading out of an electromagnetic wave as the result of encountering a surface or medium with irregularities?
- A. Diffraction
- B. Refraction
- C. Reflection
- D. Scattering

> **Official Correct Answer**: **A** —   
> **Technical Explanation**: ## D: Scattering Scattering occurs when an electromagnetic wave encounters a surface or medium with irregularities, causing the wave to spread out erratically. This can lead to signal degradation in wireless networks.

### Question 3: Which kind of service set consists of multiple APs’ coverage areas, integrated into a coherent whole offering connectivity for clients?
- A. BSS
- B. IBSS
- C. MBSS
- D. ESS

> **Official Correct Answer**: **D** — D: ESS  
> **Technical Explanation**: An extended service set (ESS) consists of multiple access points (APs) with overlapping coverage areas, integrated into a single network to provide continuous connectivity for clients as they move across different APs coverage areas.

### Question 4: Which frequency bands used by 802.11 wireless LANs offer many non-overlapping channels? (select two)
- A. 2.4 GHz
- B. 5 GHz
- C. 6 GHz

> **Official Correct Answer**: **A** —   
> **Technical Explanation**: The 5 GHz and 6 GHz frequency bands used by 802.11 wireless LANs offer many non-overlapping channels, simplifying the channel selection process compared to the 2.4 GHz band. These channels can be combined to create wider channels, enabling greater data transfer rates.


---

### Question 5: Which 2.4 GHz channel pattern is generally recommended to avoid interference between adjacent APs?
- A. 1-5-9
- B. 1-6-11
- C. 1-7-13
- D. 1-6-13

> **Official Correct Answer**: **B** — B: 1-6-11  
> **Technical Explanation**: In the 2.4 GHz band, the 1-6-11 channel pattern is generally recommended as it uses three non-overlapping channels, reducing interference between adjacent APs. However, most countries outside of the US and Canada can use other patterns as well, such as 1-5-9-13.

### Question 6: Which of the following uniquely identifies each AP in an ESS?
- A. SSID
- B. BSSID

- D. Roaming ID

> **Technical Explanation**: Each AP in an ESS is uniquely identified by its basic service set identifier (BSSID), which is the AP’s MAC address.

### Question 10: Which 802.11 standards support both the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands? (select three)
- A. 802.11be
- B. 802.11a
- C. 802.11g
- D. 802.11n
- E. 802.11ac
