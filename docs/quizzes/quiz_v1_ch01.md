# Assessment & Review: Volume 1 Chapter 1 - Chapter 1: Introduction to the CCNA

### Question 1: Which Cisco exam code currently designates the comprehensive single-exam CCNA certification?
- A. 200-125
- B. 200-301
- C. 100-105
- D. 350-401

> **Official Correct Answer**: **B** — B: 200-301  
> **Technical Explanation**: Cisco exam 200-301 is the single consolidated exam required to earn the Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA) certification, replacing older composite and two-exam paths.


---

### Question 2: What is the official duration of the CCNA 200-301 certification exam?
- A. 90 minutes
- B. 105 minutes
- C. 120 minutes
- D. 150 minutes

> **Official Correct Answer**: **C** — C: 120 minutes  
> **Technical Explanation**: The CCNA 200-301 exam is a 120-minute timed exam administered through Pearson VUE at test centers or online proctored.


---

### Question 3: Which of the six CCNA 200-301 exam blueprint domains carries the highest weight at 25%?
- A. Network Fundamentals
- B. Network Access
- C. IP Connectivity
- D. Security Fundamentals

> **Official Correct Answer**: **C** — C: IP Connectivity  
> **Technical Explanation**: Domain 3.0 (IP Connectivity) represents 25% of the CCNA exam blueprint, making it the highest-weighted single domain, followed by Network Fundamentals (20%) and Network Access (20%).


---

### Question 4: On the actual CCNA exam, what happens when you skip or answer a question?
- A. You can mark it for review and return at the end
- B. You cannot go back once you proceed to the next question
- C. You can revisit unanswered questions only
- D. You have 3 opportunities to change answers

> **Official Correct Answer**: **B** — B: You cannot go back once you proceed to the next question  
> **Technical Explanation**: In Cisco certification exams, questions are linear; once you confirm an answer or click Next, you cannot return to previous questions.
