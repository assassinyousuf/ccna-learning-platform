# Cisco CCNA 200-301 Exam Readiness & Time Protocol

A structured framework and strategic operational checklist for candidates preparing for the Cisco Certified Network Associate examination.

---

## 1. Exam Blueprint Weightings

| Domain | Exam Percentage | Key Focus Areas |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1.0 Network Fundamentals** | 20% | OSI/TCP-IP models, cabling, IPv4/IPv6, wireless architectures |
| **2.0 Network Access** | 20% | VLANs, 802.1Q, EtherChannel, Rapid PVST+, Wireless LAN Controllers |
| **3.0 IP Connectivity** | 25% | Routing table verification, static routes, single-area OSPFv2 |
| **4.0 IP Services** | 10% | NAT/PAT, NTP, DHCP, DNS, SNMP, Syslog, QoS mechanisms |
| **5.0 Security Fundamentals** | 15% | Access Control Lists (ACL), Port Security, DHCP Snooping, Dynamic ARP Inspection |
| **6.0 Automation & Programmability** | 10% | SDN architectures, REST APIs, JSON/YAML, Ansible, DNA Center |

---

## 2. Exam Day Time Management Protocol

- **Total Time Available**: 120 minutes.
- **Question Volume**: Approximately 100 to 105 questions.
- **Average Pace**: 60 to 75 seconds per standard multiple-choice question.
- **Performance Simlet Budget**: 5 to 7 minutes maximum per live CLI simulation or drag-and-drop exercise.
- **Critical Policy Note**: **No backward navigation is permitted** on the Cisco CCNA exam. Once a question is submitted, you cannot return to review or change the answer.

---

## 3. 7-Day Pre-Exam Countdown Checklist

- [ ] **T-7 Days**: Complete full 100-question mock simulation in the CCNA Platform Test Center under timed conditions.
- [ ] **T-5 Days**: Review Subnetting Magic Number calculations until subnets can be calculated within 30 seconds.
- [ ] **T-4 Days**: Practice CLI configuration of OSPFv2, PAT, and VLAN subinterfaces in Packet Tracer.
- [ ] **T-3 Days**: Memorize standard administrative distances (`Direct=0`, `Static=1`, `eBGP=20`, `OSPF=110`, `RIP=120`).
- [ ] **T-2 Days**: Review IEEE 802.11 standards, WPA3 encryption, and SDN controller northbound/southbound APIs.
- [ ] **T-1 Day**: Light review of cheat sheets; ensure valid primary and secondary government-issued photo IDs are ready.
- [ ] **Exam Day**: Arrive 30 minutes early (or log in 30 minutes early for Pearson OnVUE online proctoring).
